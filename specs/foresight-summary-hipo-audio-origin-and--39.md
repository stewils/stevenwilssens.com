## Problem

Readers: a recruiter or hiring manager reading the résumé PDF or `/experience` (skimming, often on a phone, often forwarded), the AI assistants that read Steven's profile over WebMCP (`src/webmcp.ts`) and the MCP server (`functions/_lib/mcp/tools.ts`), and a peer checking the work. `profile.summary` is on no HTML page. It reaches readers through the PDF, WebMCP, the MCP tools and the admin's LinkedIn comparison. The site's "Ask about Steven" prompt (`functions/_lib/ask/prompt.ts`) does not include it, but it does include the honors and the roles, so it gets the new honor and both new bullets.

1. **The summary lists what Steven built, not why he is hired.** Today `profile.summary` opens with "inventing products in areas I was new to" and lists domains. The two judgment calls a hiring manager for a senior product role looks for are already on the site, but buried. One is choosing low latency over 4K for xCloud (a bullet on `spatial-audio-lead`). The other is forecasting how a key Windows AI feature would land in retail (Amit Gupte's recommendation, and the `ai-feature-forecast` selected-work case). The summary is the first paragraph of the PDF and the profile summary that WebMCP and the MCP server return, and it says neither.
2. **A selection signal is missing.** Microsoft's High Potential (HiPo) program is a talent selection recruiters recognize. It appears nowhere in `honors` (4 entries today, the latest from 2012).
3. **Where the spatial audio work started is untold.** `spatial-audio-senior` (Oct 2008 - Aug 2017) says the work "culminat[ed] in the Spatial Audio Platform and Windows Sonic". It does not say Steven was the one asked to define it, which is the difference between inventing a product and shipping one.
4. **The scale of the Windows quality data in the Lead role is implicit.** `csx-data` describes the release decisions but gives no scale. The 1.6 billion figure appears only on the later `windows-devices-data` role, as "telemetry from".

How we know: Steven's approved instruction above, recorded through the MCP. He named the backing himself. The summary's two examples check out against `src/resume.ts`: the `spatial-audio-lead` bullet "Shaped Xbox xCloud game streaming strategy, prioritizing low latency over 4K resolution and making it the platform's key differentiator." and Amit Gupte's recommendation "…notably demonstrated when he accurately forecast how a key Windows AI feature would land in retail well before broad rollout." The `csx-data` bullet's source is dossier entry 50. The HiPo selection and the 2015 audio ask are Steven's own word in this instruction.

**2. How honors render, and the AC-4 fix.** `scripts/resume.mjs:161` maps each `[title, issued]` to ``${title without a leading "Microsoft "} <small>(${issued.replace(/^Issued by Microsoft · (\w+ )?/, '')})</small>``, joined with ` · `. For `'Issued by Microsoft'` the regex does not match (it needs ` · `), so the PDF would print **"Selected for Microsoft’s High Potential (HiPo) program (Issued by Microsoft)"**. The title keeps "Microsoft’s" because `^Microsoft ` does not match "Selected…", which is correct. The fix computes the date first and prints the parenthesised part only when there is one:

```js
const dated = issued.replace(/^Issued by Microsoft(?: · (?:\w+ )?)?/, '')
… `${esc(title…)}${dated ? ` <small>(${esc(dated)})</small>` : ''}`
```

"Issued by Microsoft" and "" both give no parentheses. The four dated honors give exactly the markup they give today (`… <small>(2012)</small>`), and an issuer other than Microsoft still prints in full, as it does now. This covers the designer's "empty or equal to the whole field" rule. They need no change, and "(Issued by Microsoft)" there is acceptable (AC-5).

**3. The layout ladder and page fit (AC-8).** `buildResume` renders `layouts` in order: `{2, roomy}`, `{1, roomy}`, `{1, compact}`, `{0, compact}`. No layout follows `{0, compact}`, so a PDF that is still three pages there ships as three pages, and the check fails it. - **Page 1** ends after the "Scaled experimentation adoption…" bullet of `windows-devices-data`, about 33 px above the bottom margin. The summary is 5 lines today (about 520 characters at about 118 per line) and grows by about 185 characters to about 705. That is 6 lines if it wraps tightly and 7 if it does not, at 9.5pt × 1.38, about 20 px a line in compact. With **+1 line**, page 1 absorbs it and nothing moves. With **+2 lines**, the "Scaled experimentation…" bullet (2 lines) moves to page 2 (`.role.detailed li { break-inside: avoid }`), which adds about 32 px there.
