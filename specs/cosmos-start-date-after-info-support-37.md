# Cosmos starts in Jan 2005, right after Info Support, so the early career no longer overlaps

Slug: `cosmos-start-date-after-info-support-37` · Branch: `update/cosmos-start-date-after-info-support-37` · Status: built
Asked by Steven on 2026-10-22: "Change the start date of the Software Developer role at Cosmos (role key "cosmos") from Jan 2004 to Jan 2005, so it starts right after the Info Support role ends. Keep the Cosmos end date (Dec 2005) and the Info Support dates (Jan 2001 - Dec 2004) unchanged. Apply everywhere the Cosmos dates appear (resume data in src/resume.ts, experience page, PDF). Change nothing else."

This is a one-fact change in `src/resume.ts`, plus the removal of the one hand-typed copy
of the old Cosmos timeline (the MCP interview angle, AC-9) and the tests that encoded the

no gate script changes, so `/building-this-site` and the Team page stay as they are.

## Problem

A recruiter or hiring manager reading Steven's early career, on `/experience` or in the
résumé PDF, sees two roles that overlap by a full year: Software Developer at Cosmos
(Jan 2004 - Dec 2005) and Developer and Trainer at Info Support (Jan 2001 - Dec 2004).
Overlapping dates without an explanation read as either a data error or two jobs at
once, and they invite a question in a screening call that has nothing to do with the
role being hired for.

How we know:

- The résumé check raises it on every run: `dataQuestions` in `scripts/resume-check.mjs`
  asks "Software Developer (Jan 2004 - Dec 2005) overlaps with Developer and Trainer at
  Info Support (Jan 2001 - Dec 2004). Were these consultancy placements, or should the
  dates change?", and it is listed on `/admin` → **Résumé** with the questions.
- The MCP server's recruiter lens (`recruiterLens` in `functions/_lib/mcp/tools.ts`)
  flags the `cosmos` role with "Overlaps with Developer and Trainer at Info Support", and
  its hand-written interview angle for `cosmos` tells Steven to "get the timeline
  straight".
- Two earlier specs carried it as an open question
  (`specs/building-steven-wilssens-com-merge-site--29.md`, `specs/site-update-team-runs.md`).
- Steven has now answered it himself: the dates should change, Cosmos starts Jan 2005.

## Outcome

Readers: recruiters and hiring managers first (the experience timeline and the PDF they
download or are forwarded), then Steven in `/admin`, where the overlap question
disappears from the résumé questions. Once this ships, every place the Cosmos role is
shown reads Jan 2005 - Dec 2005, the early career reads as a clean sequence
(Info Support to Dec 2004, Cosmos from Jan 2005 to Dec 2005, Compuware from Jan 2006),
and nothing else about Steven changes.
